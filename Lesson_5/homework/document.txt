Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that allows
computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

AI is used in many areas, including medicine, education,
transportation and industry.

In medicine, AI helps doctors analyze medical images
and identify diseases.

In education, AI helps students learn and provides
personalized learning experiences.

In transportation, AI is used in navigation systems
and self-driving cars.

The main advantages of AI are automation,
fast data processing and the ability to analyze
large amounts of information.
